# Clara — content and image guide

Clara is the soft feminine luxury concept: blush and cream, graceful serif typography, warm portraits and an adult, reassuring visual tone.

## Replace before client use

- **Elowen Skin Studio** is fictional; replace it with the real business identity.
- The content is illustrative only. Confirm the client’s real treatments, practitioner details, policies and action destinations before publishing.
- Remove the EB demo bar and replace or remove the demonstration dialog in `script.js` for client deployment.

## Image requirements

1. Hero portrait — 1600 × 2000 px minimum, vertical 4:5. Warmly lit adult portrait with natural expression and believable skin texture.
2. Skin-detail image — 1400 × 1400 px minimum, square. Genuine, softly lit skin detail; not a before-and-after claim.
3. Beauty portrait — 1200 × 1500 px minimum, vertical 4:5. Relaxed, self-possessed adult portrait.
4. Consultation/studio moment — 1800 × 1200 px minimum, landscape 3:2. Personal, calm appointment setting.

Use client-owned or properly licensed imagery. Do not present stock or AI imagery as real treatment results, premises or clients.
