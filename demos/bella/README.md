# Bella — content and image guide

Bella is the modern-editorial concept: a sharp black-and-white framework, oversized typography, deliberate crop relationships and a single acid-yellow accent.

## Replace before client use

- **Mira Forme** is a fictional practice name; replace it with the real client identity.
- **Copy** is realistic illustrative copy only. Confirm actual services, credentials, policies, treatment information and destinations before publishing.
- Remove the `<aside class="demo-bar">` and replace/remove the demonstration dialog behaviour in `script.js` when transferring the design to a client project.

## Image requirements

1. Hero portrait — 1600 × 2000 px minimum, vertical 4:5. Fashion-aware but natural practitioner portrait; leave usable negative space.
2. Editorial spread — 2400 × 1350 px minimum, landscape 16:9. Beauty portrait or skin detail with strong, intentional lighting.
3. Consultation portrait — 1400 × 1700 px minimum, vertical 4:5. Practitioner or consultation moment, with an uncluttered setting.

Use client-owned or properly licensed images. Do not present stock or AI imagery as real treatment results, premises or clients.
