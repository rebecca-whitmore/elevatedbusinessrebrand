# Amber — content and image guide

Amber is the quiet-luxury demonstration concept: warm neutrals, disciplined serif typography, spacious composition and a personal but composed visual tone.

## Replace before client use

- **Atelier Vale Aesthetics**: fictional practice name; replace with the client's real business name.
- **Copy**: all text is realistic illustrative content for a fictional UK aesthetics practice. Confirm the client's actual treatments, credentials, consultation process, policies and CTA destination before publication.
- **Demo bar**: remove the `<aside class="demo-bar">` when copying the concept into a client repository.
- **Demonstration script**: replace or remove `script.js` behaviours that display the demo dialog.

## Image requirements

1. Hero practitioner portrait — 1600 × 2000 px minimum, vertical 4:5. Soft neutral light; composed, natural expression; allow quiet background space.
2. Consultation space or practice detail — 1800 × 1200 px minimum, landscape 3:2. Warm, restrained interiors or a genuine practice detail; avoid busy equipment shots.
3. Closing portrait/detail — 1600 × 1200 px minimum, landscape 4:3. Warm natural skin tone, personal and understated.

Use client-owned or properly licensed imagery. Do not present stock or AI-generated people as client treatment results or real premises.
