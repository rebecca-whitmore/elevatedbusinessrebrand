Questions before you begin

The useful details.

Is this suitable for my business?+

It is designed for UK aesthetics businesses with past clients they may be able to contact appropriately by email. It is focused on aesthetics rather than general beauty services such as nails or brows.

Do I need an existing email list?+

You need existing clients’ email addresses and an appropriate basis for sending them marketing emails. The core campaign begins with suitable past clients. The welcome email and bonus training help you improve your contact routine with new clients going forward.

Can I email everyone in my client records?+

No. Having an email address in your records does not automatically mean it can be used for marketing. The kit includes a permission checklist to help you review who may be suitable to receive the campaign.

Do I need MailerLite?+

No. You can use a suitable sender inside your existing clinic software or another email platform. If you choose MailerLite, the kit points you to selected official tutorials for the relevant setup and sending tasks.

How much work will I need to do?+

The wording, follow-up structure and working examples are prepared. You will review your clients, adapt the selected templates, check the campaign, send it and manage any responses.

Will this get me bookings?+

The kit is designed to help you create appropriate returning-client conversations. Results depend on your eligible client base, their circumstances, your invitation and how you use the system. Bookings and income are not guaranteed.

How do I receive everything?+

Access is immediate. After checkout, the delivery details will be sent to the email address used for your purchase.

What implementation help is included?+

You can email one implementation question and receive one personalised video response within 24 hours. Full eligibility and submission details will be included with the product.
