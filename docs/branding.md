# Brand direction: elevatedbusiness

## Brand essence
Warm authority. A friendly, professional advisor helping established
businesses grow through sales and marketing strategy, from enquiry to
sale to repeat and referral. Ambitious and warm, never loud or garish.

## Audience
Predominantly female business owners who are already trading (aesthetics,
virtual assistants and similar service businesses). Often mobile-first and
time-poor. They want clarity and practical advice, not hype.

## Tone and feeling
- Calm, confident, spacious
- Friendly but credible: a trusted expert, not a hype marketer
- Polished and human. Not corporate-cold, not cutesy, not playful
- Plain, warm language. Short sentences.

## Colour palette
- Background (base): #FAF7F2 warm off-white. Use as the main page background.
- Text and anchors (deep neutral): #1F2A37. Use for headings, body text, and
  dark sections. Never use pure black.
- Primary action turquoise: #007F86. Use for buttons and accessible links.
- Supporting turquoise: #25C9C3. Use for small decorative details and motion.
- Pale turquoise: #D9F4F2. Use for restrained backgrounds and form highlights.
- Brand spark: #04FFF7. Use only for tiny decorative moments where it does not
  carry text or essential information.
  Keep accent use to roughly 10% of any screen. Accessibility is important.
  Plenty of warm off-white space.

## Typography
- Headings: a refined serif (Fraunces preferred, Playfair Display as
  fallback). Medium weight, generous size, tight and confident.
- Body, buttons, forms: a clean sans-serif (Inter or DM Sans). Regular
  weight, 16px minimum, comfortable line height (~1.6).
- Avoid script fonts, ultra-thin weights, and overly rounded or playful fonts.

## Layout and style
- Simple single-purpose landing page. One clear goal: join the email list.
- Large headline, short supporting line, one signup form, one button.
- Generous white space, clear hierarchy, strong contrast.
- Clean, polished design. Restrained gradients and soft shadows are welcome
  where they add focus or depth. Avoid heavy effects and large areas of neon.
- Mobile-first. Large tap targets. Readable at a glance.
- Use a simple typographic wordmark in the heading for Elevated Business in the heading serif.
- I would like a simple emblem, or icon used throughout the page, and with some gentle animation. I am happy for you to create something that represents the brand.
- Imagery: minimal, warm, natural light, real-feeling. No stock-photo
  clichés. Likely will need a portrait image of myself, you can use placeholders and indicate preferred px size.

## Industry pages (e.g. /virtualassistant, /aesthetics)
- Identical layout, fonts, and core colours to the main page.
- Only these change per niche: headline, supporting copy, any examples
  or imagery, and the swappable niche accent.
- Each page's signup form feeds a dedicated MailerLite group.

## Avoid
- Loud, neon, or pop-art colours
- Girly or twee styling (florals, script type, heavy pastels)
- Masculine tropes (rockets, arrows, aggressive urgency)
- Cluttered layouts or competing calls to action
