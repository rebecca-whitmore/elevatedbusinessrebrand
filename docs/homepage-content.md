# Elevated Business homepage content plan

Status: Copy and direction approved for implementation on 10 October 2026. MailerLite integration pending.

## Page purpose

Turn established service-business owners into email subscribers.

The page has one commercial goal: make the newsletter feel immediately relevant, genuinely useful and worth allowing into a busy inbox. Every section should move the visitor towards the same signup form. There are no competing service, booking or discovery-call CTAs.

## Recommended newsletter concept

### Working name

**The Elevated Edit**

This feels polished, concise and broad enough to cover sales, marketing, customer experience, repeat business and referrals. It also gives the signup something more desirable and memorable than simply calling it “the newsletter”.

### Core promise

Practical sales and marketing advice that helps service businesses turn attention into enquiries, enquiries into sales, and customers into repeat and referral business.

### Primary CTA

**Subscribe for free**

The hero button scrolls to the full signup form later on the page. Keeping the hero focused on one clear action gives the main promise more room to land before asking for personal details.

# Visual and production notes

## Recommended turquoise palette adjustment

Retain the emotional meaning of blue-turquoise while improving accessibility:

- **Primary action turquoise:** `#007F86`, suitable for buttons with white text.
- **Bright supporting turquoise:** `#25C9C3`, for small decorative details and the animated emblem.
- **Pale turquoise:** `#D9F4F2`, for restrained backgrounds or form highlights.
- **Deep neutral:** `#1F2A37`.
- **Warm off-white:** `#FAF7F2`.

The original `#04FFF7` can remain as a tiny brand spark within the emblem if desired, but it should not carry text or essential information.

## Emblem suggestion

A simple set of two offset rings or ripples, representing a message moving outward and creating a response. It can gently expand and settle at intervals. This connects communication, momentum and growing influence without using arrows, rockets or loud sales imagery.
