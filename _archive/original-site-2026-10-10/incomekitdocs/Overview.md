# Sales Page Overview

| Scroll order | Section | What goes here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0** | **Count-down** | Header that shows date/time the price is going to increase (to be decided) - or countdown until the increase |
| **1** | **Opening offer** | Audience identifier, **Aesthetics Income Engine**, your agreed headline, a short explanation, £27 launch price and purchase button. Clear launch-deadline placeholder beside the price. |
| **2** | **The situation they recognise** | Quiet periods, fewer returning clients and relying on more posting or discounts. Connect these to the desire for steadier income, without exaggerating their feelings. |
| **3** | **The booking opportunity** | Explain why reconnecting with existing clients is worth trying and how ongoing contact fits into a steadier booking routine. |
| **4** | **See what you’ll use** | An actual email preview and worked scenario, followed by the core inclusions. This supplies concrete evidence of usefulness. |
| **5** | **How it works** | Three steps: choose suitable clients, adapt and send, follow up and maintain contact. Mention existing software and the selected MailerLite tutorials here. |
| **6** | **Your three bonuses** | Recurring email pack, short sales training, planner/results tracker. Explain the practical benefit of each. |
| **7** | **Price, guarantee and help** | Recap the package, £27 launch price rising to £49, deadline/timer, conditional 30-day response guarantee, implementation-help route and purchase button. All undecided terms visibly marked. |
| **8** | **FAQs** | Existing-list suitability, marketing permission, software, effort involved, delivery, guarantee conditions and what help includes. Answer genuine purchase objections. |
| **9** | **Final purchase invitation** | Brief outcome reminder, price, deadline and final button. Links to full terms, privacy information and contact details below. |
