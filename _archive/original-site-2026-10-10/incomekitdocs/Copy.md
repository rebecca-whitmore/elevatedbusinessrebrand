## A few questions before you start.

### Is this suitable for my practice?

It’s designed for UK medically qualified solo aesthetics practitioners offering injectables, often alongside skin treatments, who have past clients they can appropriately contact by email.

The examples and instructions focus on returning-client conversations and ongoing contact.

### Do I need an existing email list?

You need existing clients’ email addresses and an appropriate basis for sending them marketing emails.

The core campaign starts with those clients. The bonus training also helps you make inviting clients onto your list part of your appointment routine.
