The useful details

Questions,
answered.

Is the website design really free?+

Yes. There is no charge for designing and building your website. You pay £29 per month when the site is ready to launch, covering hosting and ongoing maintenance.

What does the £29 monthly fee include?+

It includes hosting, SSL, monthly backups, maintenance and friendly ongoing support for your website.

Will I own my domain?+

Yes. You purchase your domain separately and it remains yours. I can help you connect it when your website is ready.

Can I request changes before launch?+

Absolutely. We can make unlimited edits during the build, and your website won’t launch until you’re happy with it.

Am I tied into a contract?+

No. There is no long-term contract, and you can cancel your monthly service at any time.

What do you need from me to begin?+

Start with the short application form. Basic information about your business, audience and goals is enough for me to begin shaping your first demo.
